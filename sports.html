Sports

Are you looking to find friends to run/hike/bike/explore the great outdoors together? Are you interested in playing on an intramural sports team with other members of the department? If so this is the page for you! We maintain an active presence out on the streets, trails and fields, in and around Victoria!

Sports Events

Throughout the year, we host many casual sports events. Every winter we go Skating at the Oak Bay Rec centre. Other past activities include golfing, bowling, and pickleball.

Intramural Sports

Every year we gauge interest over on our Discord channel on participation in intramural sports leagues on campus. Currently we have three active intramural teams: an outdoor 7v7 Soccer team, a softball team, and an indoor volleyball team!

If you would like to join the soccer team please reach out to Liv (omoluchi [at] uvic.ca) or Ted (tgrosson [at] uvic.ca) for the softball team. You can reach out to Sam Fielder (samuelfielder [at] uvic.ca) for the volleyball team! Alternatively, you can put a message on the #sports-chat on our discord.

Before joining the team you will need to navigate over to the signup link for UVic Intramurals and pay the registration fee after signing in with your Netlink ID (what you use to sign in to UVic services) to officially join the team! Fees for intramural are approximately $60 in the summer semester and $30 in the winter semester for registered students. Running

Do you like to run? Do you want to get into running but don’t know how/where to start? If you answered yes to either of these questions then we have the group for you! Currently you can post in the Discord looking for running buddies and in the past we have even scheduled regular runs (and sometimes even go out for a well deserved brunch afterwards!) Check out the #running channel to say hello, drop a line and connect with other running inclined PAGSApiens!

Hiking

Informal hikes are organized in the #hiking channel! Usually scheduled in the early afternoon on a Saturday or Sunday, small groups of PAGSApiens convene and head on out to one of the many convenintly located and scenic hiking spots in and around Victoria! From going up and around Mt. Doug, out to Sooke, there are plenty of trails to hit, just bring a comfortable pair of shoes or hiking boots!

Climbing

Over on the #climbing channel, a group of dedicated and intrepid climbers has organized a group that goes to Boulderhouse on Sunday mornings at 10:30 (time subject to change). Also, anyone who is looking for climbing partners or someone to hang with while going bouldering, or just someone to encourage them to try it out is invited to head on over to the channel to learn more!

And more!

Are you interested in getting like minded peers to play a sport together, such as shooting hoops over in the gym, doing a group fitness class together or just a few friends for working out more regularly? If so, head on over to the Discord to connect with your fellow fitness/sports minded peers!
