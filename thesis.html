Last updated: July 2025

UVic/the department has a strict timeline that you must follow if you want to graduate within a specific semester. Luckily, Cindy has made a very handy spreadsheet to help you figure out when these deadlines are. Contact Cindy, the Graduate Program Assistant: physgrad@uvic.ca

We have prepared a thesis template for physics and astronomy grad students. This is available on GitHub: https://github.com/PAGSA/ThesisTemplate

You may also want to use a UVic PowerPoint template for your defence presentation (or any other presentations). You can find that here.
